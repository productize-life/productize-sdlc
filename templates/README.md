# templates/

Fill-in documents for your own repo:

- **`issue-tracker.template.md`** → copy to `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md` in your repo and fill
  in: your tracker's real states (don't invent new ones), your labels, and your
  **human-approval gate** in writing. `sdlc-triage` and the wayfinding flow read this file as the
  source of truth for tracker mechanics.

Keep filled copies inside your project repo — these templates stay generic here.
